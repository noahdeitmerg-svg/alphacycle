# AlphaCycle — Brand Identity

> Single source of truth for the AlphaCycle brand. Used across landing, dashboard,
> content factory, social, favicon. Cowork 2026-06-05.

## Essence
**AlphaCycle = Bitcoin cycle intelligence in one number.** The brand feeling:
calm, precise, premium, analytical — the opposite of crypto hype. Think
"Bloomberg terminal meets Linear". A steady navigator that tells you *where you
are in the cycle* so you act on structure, not emotion.

- **Name:** AlphaCycle (Alpha = edge/outperformance · Cycle = the market cycle)
- **Tagline (primary):** *Structure before emotion.*
- **Tagline (alt):** *Know where you are in the cycle.*
- **One-liner:** Bitcoin cycle intelligence — one transparent score, five regime zones.

## Logo concept
An **orbital ring** (the cycle) with a single **glowing node** sitting on the ring —
"you are here in the cycle". Reads as a gauge / orbit / clock. Minimal, geometric,
flat. (Evolves the existing dashed-ring + teal-dot mark into a premium version.)

- **Icon-only** → favicon, app icon, social avatar.
- **Icon + wordmark** → nav, marketing, headers.

## Typography
- **Display / UI:** Instrument Sans (landing) / Outfit (dashboard) — geometric sans.
- **Data / numbers:** JetBrains Mono (landing) / DM Mono (dashboard).
- Tight tracking on headlines; mono only for scores/figures.

## Voice
Calm, declarative, evidence-led. Says "historically", "typically", "regime",
"structure". Never "BUY/SELL", never predictions, never hype. Trust is the product.
