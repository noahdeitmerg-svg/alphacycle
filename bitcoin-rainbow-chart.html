What it is

The Rainbow Chart fits a logarithmic regression to Bitcoin's price history and draws coloured bands around it — from "fire sale" (cheap) to "maximum bubble" (expensive).

The honest caveat

The bands are fitted to past price with no fundamental basis — they shift as new data arrives, and the chart is explicitly "for entertainment." A nice visual, not a model.

A better alternative
