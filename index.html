FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the ARC Index?+

ARC — the AlphaCycle Risk Composite — is a single 0–100 score that classifies where Bitcoin sits in its market cycle, across five regime zones from Deep Value to Euphoria.

How is the score calculated?+

One transparent formula blends four signals: the 200-week trend (35%), drawdown from the all-time high (30%), the Fear & Greed Index (20%) and Fed net liquidity (15%). Same method, every day, backtested to 2017.

Is this financial advice?+

No. AlphaCycle measures structural risk — it does not predict price or tell you to buy or sell. It is educational and informational only. Always do your own research.

What data sources do you use?+

Bitcoin price and OHLC from Kraken, sentiment from the Fear & Greed Index, and liquidity from the Federal Reserve (WALCL, TGA, RRP). All primary, public sources.

How accurate is the model?+

Every major cycle top and bottom since 2017 falls in the expected zone in backtest. ARC lags by design — it measures the present accurately rather than guessing the future.

Would following ARC have beaten just holding?+

In backtest, by a wide margin. One rule — all-in at ARC 25, all-out at ARC 75, hold the middle — turned $10,000 into about $584,000 since 2017, versus roughly $142,000 for buy-and-hold, at −36% max drawdown instead of −84%. It took six trades in nine years. This is an in-sample backtest (the edge holds across a wide range of thresholds, not one magic number); past performance does not guarantee future results.

Yes — Pro is monthly with no lock-in, cancel anytime. And during launch, everything is free.
