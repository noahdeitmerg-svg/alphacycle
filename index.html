HOW THE ARC INDEX WORKS

The ARC combines four structural indicators — 200-week moving average deviation (35%), drawdown from ATH (25%), macro liquidity from the Fed balance sheet (25%), and market sentiment via Fear & Greed (15%) — into a single score from 0 to 100. Lower scores = lower risk = better entry. Higher scores = higher risk = reduce exposure.
