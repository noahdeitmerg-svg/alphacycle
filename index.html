FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is the ARC Index?+

ARC — the AlphaCycle Risk Composite — is a single 0–100 score that classifies where Bitcoin sits in its market cycle, across five regime zones from Deep Value to Euphoria.

How is the score calculated?+

One transparent formula blends four signals: the 200-week trend (35%), drawdown from the all-time high (30%), the Fear & Greed Index (20%) and Fed net liquidity (15%). Same method, every day, backtested to 2017.

Is this financial advice?+

No. AlphaCycle measures structural risk — it does not predict price or tell you to buy or sell. It is educational and informational only. Always do your own research.

What data sources do you use?+

Bitcoin price and OHLC from Kraken, sentiment from the Fear & Greed Index, and liquidity from the Federal Reserve (WALCL, TGA, RRP). All primary, public sources.

How accurate is the model?+

Every major cycle top and bottom since 2017 falls in the expected zone in backtest. ARC lags by design — it measures the present accurately rather than guessing the future.

Would following ARC have beaten just holding?+

In backtest, by a wide margin. The adaptive ladder — accumulate when ARC is cheap, scale to fully invested near the cycle bottom, and take profit in steps as it approaches euphoria — turned $10,000 into roughly $550,000 since 2017, versus about $147,000 for buy-and-hold, at −48% max drawdown instead of −84%. This is an in-sample backtest (the edge holds across a wide range of thresholds, not one magic number); past performance does not guarantee future results.

How is this different from free tools like CBBI?+

Free indices hand you a number and leave the hard part — what to actually do — to you. AlphaCycle adds the missing half: a transparent buy/hold/sell rule, a live demo account with a real dated track record, and a plain-English daily read. The score is free; Pro sells the decision.

Do I need a wallet or to connect funds?+

No. AlphaCycle never touches your coins, keys or exchange. It is a read-only signal you check — nothing to connect, nothing at risk.

I'm a beginner — is this for me?+

Especially. The whole point is one number plus a plain-English "today's read," so you don't need to decode fifty charts. If you can read a speedometer, you can read ARC.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes — Pro is monthly with no lock-in, cancel anytime. And during launch, everything is free.
